Version 1, 2026-11-13. These terms cover the warnahead deadline dataset (`deadlines.json`, served at
`https://warnahead.pages.dev/data/v1/deadlines.json`, kept in the public GitHub repository and
bundled with the package), the site at `https://warnahead.pages.dev` and its calendar feeds,
together "the Data". The command-line tool and the Action are covered by [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

**In short:** read it, link to it, subscribe to the calendars and quote single entries with a link.
Do not copy it in bulk. This short summary is not part of the terms; the sections below are.
